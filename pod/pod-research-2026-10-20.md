## Why the others fail

- **Lulu** — coil bound caps at **470 pages** (zodiac, flagship, and 2028 all die), and no bundling/kitting — book ships alone.
- **KDP** — no coil binding at all, premium-color page caps, no kitting. Already ruled out by the $28.87 price-floor math on just the sampler.
- **Acutrack** — runner-up: does bundling/collateral and all bindings, but it's forecast/inventory printing (best above ~50 orders/month per their own chart), not true one-off POD. Keep as the scale-up option once volume exists.
- **48 Hour Books** — coil available (+~60¢/book from earlier research) but short-run printer, no customer fulfillment/kitting program like Vervante's.
