THE IDEA

Structure that supports you.
Never a cage.

The script says: be busy, be available, be impressive. Off*Script says: be gloriously, unapologetically you—with a plan that can keep up.

Keep what helps.

Structure should support your choices, not control them. Use the pages that make life easier; leave the rest.

Follow a little curiosity.

A new route, a better question, a small adventure—tiny disruptions count.

Be honest, not perfect.

The point isn’t to perform a better life. It’s to notice the one you’re living.

Khaos isn’t a failure to get organized. It’s information.

No gold stars for burnout. No guilt for skipping a page. Done is better than perfect.
