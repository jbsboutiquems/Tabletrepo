Do you sell printed planners?

Print is offered for quarterly editions only — spiral-bound, printed on demand in the Shopify store. Yearly editions are digital only. Here is why:

Binding limits. Print-on-demand presses can bind up to roughly 740 pages. A yearly Off*Script edition runs 798 to 1,253 pages — it physically does not fit in one printed volume. A quarter is about 200–230 pages and binds beautifully.

Print cost. On-demand printing charges per page, and full color adds up fast. A quarter prints at a price that makes sense. A printed yearly would cost more to manufacture than the digital edition sells for — the price would have to triple, for the same book.

Shipping. An 800-page letter-size book is a brick: expensive to ship and easy to damage. Quarters ship like normal books.

So the lineup is simple: every quarter, in print or PDF; every year, in PDF. (You can always print your own PDF yourself, too.)
